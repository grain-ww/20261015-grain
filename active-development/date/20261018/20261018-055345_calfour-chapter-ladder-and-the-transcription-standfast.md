## The ladder

A chapter on calfour is 2,048 rounds, four equinoxes of 512. Each equinox is four journeys of 128, each journey four
quests of 32, and each quest four orbits of 8. The rungs are the four equinoxes, and one rung ends where the next begins.

| Rung | Counts | State |
|---|---|---|
| Chapter one, equinox one | 1 to 512 | open; journey two is in quest four |
| Chapter one, equinox two | 513 to 1,024 | not planned |
| Chapter one, equinox three | 1,025 to 1,536 | not planned |
| Chapter one, equinox four | 1,537 to 2,048 | not planned |

Only the current equinox is planned. The later rungs are written when they open, as the calfour rule asks.

rounds run on their themes, and the subject of each is chosen when its count arrives, not before.

- **How much.** 292 pages, batched at about twenty pages a round. That is roughly fifteen rounds of reading, and the
