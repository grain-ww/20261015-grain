# Open question -- what "at the Door" means, and three suggestions

**Kind:** open question with suggestions. It changes nothing; it waits on a word.
**Room:** the register name and the setting name share one phrase, and that is the question.

## The question

Gauge has three settings: Door, Field, and Meter ([`context/styles/gauge.md`](../../../context/styles/gauge.md)).
After the register rename in `168741b`, three tutorials read "Radha at the Door setting". That line puts a register
name in front of a setting name, so a reader can take "Door" for a register, or take "Radha" for a setting.

Two readings compete:

1. **Door is a setting only.** The register stays a separate word, and the line says the two plainly: a register, then
   a setting, each named once.
2. **Door is a register too.** Then "Door" needs its own page, and the setting names become a sub-part of it.

The room rules also use "at the door" as an ordinary phrase, for the concrete fact taken in first
(`.claude/rules/rota-and-calfive.md`, `context/TWO_ROOMS.md`). The phrase and the setting share a word, so a search for
"Door" finds both.

## Suggestions

1. **Keep Door as a setting, and say it once.** Change the style line to name the register and the setting in two
   fields, so no single phrase carries both.
2. **Extract the sameness.** The voices, styles, and tutorials repeat the same style line. One index in
   `context/styles/lib/` could hold the register names and settings, and each page would point to it. This is the
   single-strand rule applied to the style lines.
3. **Shrink the tree, after a word.** The tree's root and its living pages are the next thing to thin. The tablecloth
   audit is still held, and the moves need a word.

## What the question does not settle

- **"Tree mitra shrink."** The word *mitra* appears once in this tree, in a research file name. Its meaning here is not
  set, so this note does not act on it. If it names a specific shrink, name the target and the note can take it up.
- **The reviewer's read** for the Radha register name is still open, as recorded in the molt note.
