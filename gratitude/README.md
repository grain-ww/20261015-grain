# Gratitude -- a reading library

**Status:** Living -- the reading library and its attribution.

We study the world with thanks and keep our own prose about it, with attribution. We learn
concepts and leave third-party code where it lives; only understanding crosses into our own
work, always in words rather than copied source.

This tree carries a curated handful of the study notes the near direction leans on: Simple
Made Easy (one fact, one place), [the Toyota production system](toyota-production-system.md) (stop the line for a red), seL4
and Microkit (the proved microkernel Caravan will stand on), Buckminster Fuller's tensegrity
(the fascia image), Andrew Kelley's Zig (Rye's substrate), and the durability study (the long
return). The wider library, and the third-party submodules, stay in the 20260618-grain field;
`active-designing/` lists what is referenced but not yet carried here.
