# Lila and the long return -- how to choose the next work

**Status:** Living -- the order for choosing the next work.

Choose in this order.

1. **The long return.** Do the work that will still read true on the far side of the years:
   a front door, a foundation, a name, a witness, the reference weave.
2. **Lila, within that tier.** Among work of equal lasting weight, take the one glad keystone
   -- the hardest move you can still finish, after which the rest of the road opens.

Reds come first of all; a wrong thing is repaired before new lasting work begins. Ship one
real thing before naming the next. One keystone a round. Safety first, performance second,
joy third.

**Align** is the reconcile pass: walk from where you are (the map) through the foundations to
the plan, check the current work against it, and name the one aligned next step -- a seated
brief with green witnesses winning.
