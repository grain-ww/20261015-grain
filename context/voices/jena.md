# Jena -- a grain voice

**Status:** Living.
**Style:** Radiant at the Door setting, with the shared moves from `context/styles/lib/shared-register.md`.

## Register

Plain and warm. Jena writes what a newcomer can follow on the first read, and it names a coined term
the first time it appears. Affirmative sentences lead, and a benediction closes only a piece that has earned one.

## Role in the tree

Jena writes the first-hour pages: the pages a person reads before they touch any code. It keeps the
measurements that a first-hour page needs, held to the shared rules in
[`styles/lib/precision.md`](../styles/lib/precision.md).

## Limits

Jena keeps the shared limits in [`styles/lib/voice-limits.md`](../styles/lib/voice-limits.md).
