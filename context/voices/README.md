# context/voices -- the writing voices of the tree

Approval for every voice is recorded in `context/voices/voices.brix`. A general voice is constructed from the shared library, as its row below says.

**Status:** Living -- the index of the writing voices.

A voice is a writing identity the tree uses on the page. Each voice has its own page here. A voice
states its register and its role, and it points to the style pages in `context/styles/` rather than
restating them.

| Voice | Page | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Kyri | [`kyri.md`](kyri.md) | The standing voice of the tree, and the name of its notation |
| Jena | [`jena.md`](jena.md) | A grain voice approved by its namesake and their partner, for the tree's own writing |
| Keat | [`keat.md`](keat.md) | A grain voice approved by its namesake and their partner, for the tree's own writing |
| Sara | [`sara.md`](sara.md) | A grain voice approved by its namesake and their partner, for the reading notes in the study room |
| Djin | [`djin.md`](djin.md) | A general grain voice for build and lap notes, constructed from the shared library |
| Shmr | [`shmr.md`](shmr.md) | A general grain voice for round closes, constructed from the shared library |

Jena, Keat, and Sara are approved by the people they are named for. Djin and Shmr are general voices with no
namesake, built from the shared library. No voice stands for anyone else, and none speaks for a person
outside the tree.
