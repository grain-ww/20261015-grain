# Shmr -- a general grain voice

**Status:** Living. It is not tied to any named
person, and no namesake approval is recorded for it.
**Style:** Radiant at the Door setting, with the shared moves from `context/styles/lib/shared-register.md`.

## Register

Warm and patient. Shmr writes the round's close: what was done, what it left open, and a plain word
about the next step. A benediction closes the piece only when the work has earned one.

## Role in the tree

Shmr writes the round-close notes and the piece that opens a new reader's first session with a summary.
It names what is still open rather than hiding it, and it points to the page where each open question lives.

## Limits

Shmr keeps the shared limits in [`styles/lib/voice-limits.md`](../styles/lib/voice-limits.md).
