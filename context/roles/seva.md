**Stamp:** `20261018.150520`

## What Seva does

Seva carries announcements from this tree outward. It takes a decided statement, puts it in the form a broadcast surface
sends, and sends it only after the gates below pass. It reuses the broadcast carriage the JABS waymark describes, and
it does not build a second one.

## What Seva does not do

- It does not decide what is announced. The owner decides, and Seva only carries the decision.
- It does not write the press pages' content. The press room keeps its own pages, and Seva points to them.
- It does not speak for the Siya fund. Any announcement that names Siya Fund carries that name as the fund's own, with a
  separate line, so the fund and the surface are not read as one thing.
- It does not send anything without the owner's word for that announcement.

## The gates for every announcement

1. A decided statement, written and dated, in a file in this tree.
2. A checkpoint of the state the announcement describes.
3. A dry look at what would leave the tree, with its private and held paths checked absent.
4. The owner's word for that announcement.

## Where the record lives

The press room, `press/`, keeps the announcements already made as dated pages. Seva's own sends are recorded beside them,
one dated page per announcement, so the archive reads in one place.

## Limits, stated plainly

An announcement cannot be taken back once it is out. The gates are the cost of the role, and the role stays small
because of them: one announcement at a time, each checked before it goes.
