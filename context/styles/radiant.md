# Radiant Style -- the warm register Gauge inherits

**Status:** Living -- the warm register Gauge inherits.

Radiant is the warmest register and the voice this tree writes in. Gauge borrows its
warmth and adds a number that can be checked.

## What Radiant is

Radiant writes toward the reader. It opens with what the work does, speaks to someone who may be meeting it for
the first time, and lets gratitude show where it is owed. Its warmth comes from attention rather than from
adjectives: a warm sentence names one specific thing, and a generous one leaves room for the reader's own question.

## Core moves and the checklist

The core moves and the pre-publish checklist are stated once, in
[`lib/shared-register.md`](lib/shared-register.md). Radiant keeps its warmth; the shared moves live
in the library, so a change to them happens in one place.

Radiant is composed from the library: [`lib/shared-register.md`](lib/shared-register.md) for the moves, and
[`lib/precision.md`](lib/precision.md) for measured claims. Its sister register, [`radha.md`](radha.md), is composed from the
same library and adds the rule that its sources are the passed studies.
