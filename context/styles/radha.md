# Radha Style -- devotion as love, written from passed studies

**Status:** Living. A style composed from the shared library, not a copy of it.
**Composed from:** [`lib/shared-register.md`](lib/shared-register.md) (the core moves and the checklist),
[`lib/devotional-register.md`](lib/devotional-register.md) (sacred texts and their review), and
[`lib/voice-limits.md`](lib/voice-limits.md) (what every voice keeps out).
**Sources:** [`../../study/MANIFEST.md`](../../tablecloth/study/MANIFEST.md) -- the accepted texts. Radha quotes and cites only those.

## What Radha adds to the shared moves

- **Love first, then the argument.** A Radha page names the affection the work serves before it names the work's method.
  Prema bhakti is devotion as love, and the page says so in plain words.
- **Practice, named.** A Radha page says what a person does, step by step, and what they feel is left to the reader.
- **Passed studies only.** Any text the page draws on is one the study manifest accepts. The page names it by its
  manifest row. A text the manifest does not accept is not quoted, not paraphrased as if it were studied, and not
  attributed.
- **Fewer claims.** Radha makes fewer claims than Radiant, and each one carries its source. A claim about a tradition's
  practice or history is named with its source, or left out.

## What Radha keeps from Radiant

Lead with what is. Active voice. Prefer "yet" and "however" to "but." Vary sentence length. Name each coined term on its
first use. Let a benediction close only a piece that has earned one. These moves live in the shared library, and Radha
takes them from there.
