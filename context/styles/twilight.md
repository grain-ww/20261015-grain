# Twilight -- Radiant at dusk

**Status:** Living. The nocturne register for the rare piece where reverence serves better than brightness.
**Style:** Radiant's own warmth, slowed, with the shared moves from [`lib/shared-register.md`](lib/shared-register.md)
and the precision rules from [`lib/precision.md`](lib/precision.md).

## What Twilight keeps from Radiant

Everything Radiant asks still holds: lead with what is, speak actively, prefer `yet` to `but`, and let each sentence
land. Twilight changes the pace and the light, not the claims.

## What Twilight adds

- **Calm, never gloom.** The piece is low and gentle, and it stays affirmative. A line that reads heavy or defeated
  has left the register.
- **Aim, then dissolve.** A line names the one thing meant, and the next line opens it outward.

## When to use it

For a devotional or contemplative piece, a founding passage, or a closing that has been earned. The default register
stays Radiant. Twilight is the threshold, not the room.

## What Twilight does not change

Numbers, paths, dates, and proper names are held exactly, as in any register. The precision rules in `lib/precision.md`
apply to a Twilight piece without exception.
