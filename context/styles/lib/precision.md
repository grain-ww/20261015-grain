# Precision -- the checkable core of every voice

**Status:** Living. The shared precision rules. The reasoning behind the first rules is in
[`../../TAME_CORE.md`](../../TAME_CORE.md), reflexes 2 and 4, and in the figure rule of
[`../gauge.md`](../gauge.md).

- **Every figure carries its unit, its date, its source, and what holds it still.** A figure
  without these is a rumour with a number. Pinned, walled, or free: say which.
- **Every claim is one a reader can check.** State it as what must hold, not as a hope.
- **Say why beside each surprising choice, threshold, or number.** The reason stays with the thing.
- **Bound the claim.** Name the scope, the period, and the assumptions before the number.
