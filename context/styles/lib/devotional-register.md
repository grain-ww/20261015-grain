# Devotional register -- sincere, plain, and sourced

**Status:** Living. A register for devotional and contemplative pieces. It shares the moves in
[`shared-register.md`](shared-register.md) and adds the rules below. Its name is a working name. A name
drawn from a devotional figure waits for the owner's word and a reviewer's read, under the tree's rule on naming, which
keeps a person or a tradition out of a path or a page.

## What the register keeps

- **Sincerity over ornament.** Write what is meant, in plain words a practitioner would use.
- **Practice, named.** Say what a person does, step by step, rather than what they feel.
- **Humility about the source.** Say whose words they are, and keep every claim within what the source states.
- **Promises only what the tradition makes.** A devotional piece states only the results its source states.

## Sacred texts

- A scripture passage is quoted only with its source, its verse reference, and its translator named.
- A quoted passage waits, outside ad copy, scripts, and public pages, until a reviewer who knows the
  tradition has read it and the owner has agreed. The review request in
  `tablecloth/documents/notes/date/20261018/` is the first example of that process.
- A sacred text stays out of sales lines and products.

## Where the register stops

A devotional piece is a practice, and a teaching about a tradition belongs in a different register. Where a claim about a tradition's practice or
history needs a source, the source is named or the claim is left out.
