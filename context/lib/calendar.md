## The elder's rings

The elder's five rings are a deferred reference, held in [`../../yonder/calfive/elder-rings.md`](../../yonder/calfive/elder-rings.md). The tree's chapter is the calfour chapter of 2,048 rounds, and this page does not use the elder's.

## Calfour, as a proposal

Calfour starts with an orbit of eight rounds, and each larger ring is four times the one before. The metaphors keep the
same shape as the elder's, but the multiple is four, not five.

| Ring | Rounds | Built as |
|---|---|---|
| Round | 1 | the unit |
| Orbit | 8 | the base |
| Quest | 32 | 4 orbits |
| Journey | 128 | 4 quests |
| Equinox | 512 | 4 journeys |
| Chapter | 2,048 | 4 equinoxes |

The base is the rota of eight. The 405 idea of the elder, a point count of 15 times 3 times 9, is not carried over.
