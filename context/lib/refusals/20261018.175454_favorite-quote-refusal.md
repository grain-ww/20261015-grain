# Refusal -- a passage filed as a favorite quote of ours

**Stamp:** `20261018.175454` -- **Kind:** Refusal record.

**What was offered.** A passage from Whitman's "Song of Myself" (eight lines from the stored EPUB, beginning "I lean and loafe at my ease") was presented in the reply as a favorite passage of this tree.

**Outcome.** Refused as a favorite. The passage is in the study room as a stored text, and it is not filed as a favorite quote of the tree. The tree has not chosen it.

**Why.** A favorite is a choice the owner makes. Quotes in this tree come from texts the study manifest accepts, and each is read and cited from its row. Calling one ours claims a choice that was not made.

**Stands.** The passage stays readable in the study room. Its reading in the reply is a reading, not a favorite.
