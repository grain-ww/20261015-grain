# Reading the stored texts -- a numbered study course

**Stamp:** `20261018.173612` -- **Style:** Jena at the Door setting, with Radha commentary -- **Status:** Living, draft course.

Four lessons, read in order. Each lesson has a Jena section, which teaches the reading, and a Radha section, which names the affection the reading serves and the practice a reader can do. Radha quotes only texts the study manifest accepts, and each quote carries its manifest row.

| Lesson | Text | Manifest row |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | How a text enters the room | the gate, every row |
| 02 | Reading a poem's first lines | Walt Whitman, Leaves of Grass (1892) |
| 03 | Reading a preface | Thomas Taylor, Plato (1793) |
| 04 | Reading work that is done with the hands | Walt Whitman, Leaves of Grass (1892) |

The lessons draw on the study note in `study/texts/whitman-leaves-of-grass-1892/study-note.md`, and on the draft transcription in `study/texts/taylor-plato-wellcome-1793/draft/`. The Taylor draft is one reading by eye. Its quotes are marked as draft.
