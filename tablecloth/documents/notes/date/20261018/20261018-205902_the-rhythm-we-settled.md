## Rules of the rhythm

1. **Measure before moving.** A read-only scan or a listing comes first. Every change in this session followed a number someone read.
2. **Ask for the word on anything that leaves the tree.** A push, a publish, or a rebase onto a peer's remote waits for a yes. A chain of spell letters does not count as that yes.
3. **Fast-forward only.** A push that needs a force is refused, and a divergence is settled by a rebase onto the remote head, then a plain push.
4. **Write the mechanism first.** Every commit body names the file or function in plain words before any image. The commit hook refuses a body without one, and it is right to.
5. **A pin moves whole or not at all.** A living pin that nears its byte bound sheds a whole section to an archive file, with a pointer left behind. Nothing is cut.
6. **Name what was not recorded.** A checkpoint row says when a head was not read, rather than naming one from memory.
