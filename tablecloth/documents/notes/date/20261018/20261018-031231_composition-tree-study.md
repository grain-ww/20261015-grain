## What this tree is

This is a small place for building one thing at a time. Its near aim is a supervisor called Caravan, written in Rye,
and meant to stand on a proved microkernel. Nothing like that is built yet. Today the tree holds the rules for building,
the reasons under the work, and one small program that has been checked to run.

## How the tree is laid out

There are eighteen entries at the root. Most of the weight sits in a few rooms: the foundations explain the reasons,
the context pages state the rules that stay the same, and the tablecloth holds documents that are not public. The
design writing lives in `active-designing/`, and the work in progress in `active-development/`.

Each dated file carries its own stamp in its name, on one clock, read in New York time. A living page carries no stamp,
and it is edited in place. A dated record keeps its words, and a correction goes in an errata beside it.

## How a round works

A round is one bounded step of work, ending in one commit. Its number comes from the count of commits, which is why the
count is printed in every status reading. At commit count 103 the tree was in orbit seven, round twelve. Every round
checks its own links before it closes, and the check reads a hundred and sixteen links, none broken.

## What keeps it honest

Several guards stand in the tree, and each one is a file you can read:

- The study gate. A text enters the study room only with a public domain or CC0 statement from its own page. Seven
  records have passed, and each one quotes its statement. Refusals are kept too, so a reader sees why a text is absent.
- The link check, in shell and in Rishi.
- The bounds witness, which refuses any compiler that is not the pinned version.
- The removal verbs. Molt prepares and cuts nothing. Breach renames and keeps the history. Debride rewrites history,
  and it needs a named target and a clear word first.

The commit-message wall that the field tree carries is not wired into this tree yet, so a commit here carries its
mechanism by the rule in `.claude/rules/`, not by a hook.

## What is still open

Several things wait on a word from the owner: the push to the second private remote, the reading of the license by
licensed counsel, and the choice of the register name. A few removals are prepared and wait too. Nothing in this
composition changes any of them.

## For a first reader

Start with the first-hour tutorial. Then read the guards, then the round steps, then the link check in Rishi. The
reading path is in `docs-geode/README.md`, and each step there says what you should be able to do afterward.
