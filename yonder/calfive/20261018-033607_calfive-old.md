## Calfive -- the round load

Calfive is the calendar in use now. A proposed successor, calfour, is awaiting the owner's decisions on its hierarchy and on
whether it replaces Calfive. Until then, the formulas below are the live ones, and nothing else in the tree changes them.


The orbit round is `(count - 1) % 15 + 1`; the quest place is `(count - 1) % 75 + 1`. The
five goals, by element, are aether heart, water aesthetics, fire talent, earth law, air fun.
Earth's three signs carry what must stop -- Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn -- so let that verb color
the keystone. A session log may record `orbit round N sign -- the verb` beside the rota row.

The calendar rings are counted capacities, never labels: orbit 15, quest 75, journey 375,
equinox 1,875, chapter 9,375 rounds. A plan's parts are movements, marked by stamp and name.
