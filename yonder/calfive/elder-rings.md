## The elder's rings, as deferred reference

The elder's panchanga has five rings, each five times the one before, from a base of fifteen rounds:

| Ring | Rounds | Built as |
|---|---|---|
| Orbit | 15 | the base |
| Quest | 75 | 5 orbits |
| Journey | 375 | 5 quests |
| Equinox | 1,875 | 5 journeys |
| Chapter | 9,375 | 5 equinoxes |

Source: the panchanga foundation in the field tree, and the ring note in the field's stamp-and-name rule.
