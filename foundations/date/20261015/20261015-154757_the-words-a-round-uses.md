# The words a round uses

- **arc** -- a long body of related work, spanning many sessions.
- **round** -- one bounded session, with an opening and a close.
- **lap** -- one complete step inside a round, owing the lap before it nothing.
- **ladder** -- a sequence of steps that genuinely run in a load-bearing order.
- **rung** -- one step of a ladder where a real ladder exists.
- **fold** -- to file something into its dated shelf.
- **lift** -- to take a thing out of a ladder.
- **carry** -- to bring a fact or a key forward into a new place.
- **delegate** -- to hand a bounded task to another hand.
- **fascia** -- the connective tissue of references; its health is a measured grade.
